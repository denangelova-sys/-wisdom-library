Seneca — Quotes and Wisdom

Lucius Annaeus Seneca (c. 4 BCE–65 CE) was a Roman Stoic philosopher, dramatist, and statesman. His essays and letters examine time, anger, adversity, wealth, friendship, death, and the deliberate cultivation of character.
