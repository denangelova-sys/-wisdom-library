Julius Caesar — Quotes and Wisdom

Julius Caesar (100–44 BCE) was a Roman general, statesman, and author. His surviving Commentaries provide firsthand Roman accounts of military campaigns, while later authors preserve many sayings attributed to him. Popular quotations therefore require careful source distinction.
