Cicero — Quotes and Wisdom

Marcus Tullius Cicero (106–43 BCE) was a Roman statesman, orator, and philosopher. His philosophical writings adapted Greek ideas for Roman audiences and explored duty, friendship, justice, virtue, and public life.
