Aristotle — Quotes and Wisdom

Aristotle (384–322 BCE) was a Greek philosopher whose work ranges across ethics, politics, logic, natural philosophy, rhetoric, and biology. His Nicomachean Ethics develops an influential account of virtue, practical judgment, flourishing, and habituated action.
