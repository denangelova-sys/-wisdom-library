Plato — Quotes and Wisdom

Plato (c. 428/427–348/347 BCE) was an Athenian philosopher and student of Socrates. His dialogues explore justice, knowledge, virtue, education, politics, love, and the nature of reality. The Republic is among the central works represented in this collection.
