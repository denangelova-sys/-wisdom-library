Marcus Aurelius — Quotes and Wisdom

Marcus Aurelius (121–180 CE) was Roman emperor and a major Stoic thinker. His Meditations are private philosophical exercises on judgment, discipline, mortality, duty, and living in accordance with nature. Wisdom Library distinguishes passages presented as verified quotations from editorial renderings, and links the collection back to the cited classical source.
