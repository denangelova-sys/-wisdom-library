Peter Deunov — Quotes and Wisdom

Peter Deunov (Beinsa Douno, 1864–1944) was a Bulgarian spiritual teacher and founder of the Universal White Brotherhood movement. His teachings discuss love, wisdom, truth, inner development, music, nature, and spiritual practice. Wisdom Library keeps his material distinct from ancient classical source traditions.
