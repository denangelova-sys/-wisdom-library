Sun Tzu — Quotes and Wisdom

Sun Tzu, traditionally dated to the late Zhou period, is the name associated with The Art of War. The text treats strategy, preparation, information, adaptability, positioning, and the avoidance of unnecessary conflict. Many popular quotations are paraphrases, so source labeling matters.
