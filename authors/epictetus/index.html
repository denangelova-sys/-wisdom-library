Epictetus — Quotes and Wisdom

Epictetus (c. 50–c. 135 CE) was a Stoic teacher whose surviving teachings emphasize the distinction between what is within our control and what is not. His practical philosophy focuses on judgment, character, freedom, and disciplined response to events.
