Confucius — Quotes and Wisdom

Confucius (551–479 BCE) was a Chinese philosopher associated with an ethical tradition centered on learning, ritual, humane conduct, family responsibility, and cultivated character. The Analects preserve sayings and conversations attributed to him and his followers.
