Plutarch — Quotes and Wisdom

Plutarch (c. 46–after 119 CE) was a Greek writer and philosopher whose Parallel Lives and Moralia examine character, ethics, politics, education, and human conduct. His works are an important source for understanding how ancient writers used biography and moral reflection together.
