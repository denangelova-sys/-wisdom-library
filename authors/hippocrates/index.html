Hippocrates — Quotes and Wisdom

Hippocrates is the traditional name associated with the ancient Greek medical tradition represented by the Hippocratic Corpus. The corpus contains works on observation, prognosis, regimen, disease, and medical ethics; individual sayings should therefore be attributed carefully rather than treated as unquestionably verbatim words of one historical author.
