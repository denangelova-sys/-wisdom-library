Laozi — Quotes and Wisdom

Laozi is the traditional figure associated with the Dao De Jing, a foundational Chinese philosophical text concerned with the Dao, simplicity, non-forcing, humility, and the limits of deliberate control. The historical identity and dating of Laozi remain subjects of scholarly debate.
