Socrates — Quotes and Wisdom

Socrates (469–399 BCE) left no writings of his own. What is known about his philosophy comes chiefly from later authors, especially Plato and Xenophon, with additional comic and historical portrayals. Quotations attributed to Socrates therefore require special caution about the intermediary source.
