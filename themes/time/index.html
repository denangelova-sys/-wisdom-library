Time Quotes and Wisdom

Time is one of the most persistent subjects in wisdom literature: its scarcity, our attention to it, and the difference between being busy and living deliberately.

“Do not live as though you had endless time.”

“A life spent chasing appearances can pass before the person ever asks what is truly worth pursuing.”

“A single excellent act does not make a life excellent; happiness belongs to a whole life lived well.”

Aristotle · Nicomachean Ethics, Book I · Faithful rendering

“It is not that we have too little time; we lose much of what we are given.”

“Age changes the body, but it need not diminish the value of a life devoted to learning and service.”

“Speed matters when it preserves initiative, but haste without preparation creates vulnerability.”
