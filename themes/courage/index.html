Courage Quotes and Wisdom

Courage is presented as disciplined action in the presence of difficulty, uncertainty, or fear. It is closely connected with judgment, responsibility, and virtue.

What is courage in this collection?

Courage is treated as acting according to reason or principle even when fear, uncertainty or social pressure makes that difficult.
